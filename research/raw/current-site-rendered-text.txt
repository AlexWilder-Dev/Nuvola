Our Story

Nuvola was born from the dream of Fatjon, a pizzaiolo who honed his craft in Naples before moving to London in 2018. After years of experience in traditional Neapolitan pizzerias, he decided to bring an authentic yet innovative pizza experience to the heart of the city.

The name Nuvola (Italian for "cloud") reflects our philosophy: a light, soft, and highly digestible dough that feels like a cloud with every bite. We use the biga method, an evolution of sourdough, perfected through countless trials until we achieved the ideal balance of fragrance, texture, and flavor.

Every pizza at Nuvola is crafted with care, from the long fermentation of the dough to the selection of fresh, high-quality ingredients. Our goal is not just to serve pizza, but to share the warmth, conviviality, and lightness of the true Neapolitan tradition.

Nuvola is more than a pizzeria: it's where passion meets technique, where tradition embraces innovation, and where every slice tells a story that began in Naples and continues in London.
